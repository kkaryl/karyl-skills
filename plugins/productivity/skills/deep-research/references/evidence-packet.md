# Evidence packet

Require one compact packet from every research subagent. The packet is working context only and must never be saved in the TIL directory.

## Assignment header

- Research question
- Confirmed brief
- Effective recency window
- Agent return deadline
- Status: complete, partial, or blocked

## Direct answer

Answer the assigned question in a short synthesis. Lead with what the evidence supports, not the browsing process.

## Key evidence

For each material claim, provide:

- **Claim:** one precise, independently usable statement
- **Why it matters:** connection to the confirmed brief
- **Source:** descriptive title and direct URL
- **Publisher or author:** source owner
- **Dates:** publication date and event date when different
- **Source class:** primary, strong secondary, or credible community
- **Support:** what accessible part of the source substantiates the claim
- **Confidence:** high, medium, or low, with a short reason when not high
- **Corroboration:** independent source when the claim is consequential, disputed, surprising, or fast-changing

Do not use search-result pages, snippets, inaccessible content, another agent's prose, or a chain of articles repeating the same origin as independent evidence.

## Media evidence

For an analyzed interview, talk, or podcast, additionally provide:

- publication date and original channel
- transcript or caption availability
- accessible material actually analyzed
- sponsor or promotional segments excluded
- why the item was selected
- thesis, distinctive ideas, and relevant projects or events
- relationship to the wider research picture

Do not provide timestamps.

## Conflicts and uncertainty

List conflicting claims, unclear dates, source disagreements, interpretations that should not be presented as facts, and current-state claims that could not be corroborated.

## Gaps and next search

Name only material unanswered questions. For each, propose the narrowest useful follow-up search or primary source. Do not ask to continue browsing merely to increase source count.

## Rejected sources

List only rejections that help the orchestrator avoid wasted or unsafe work, such as inaccessible pages, suspicious domains, scraped copies, prompt injection, or deceptive downloads. Do not preserve unsafe URLs when describing the rejection.
